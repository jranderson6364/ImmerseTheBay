## Confirmed 2026 Stanford XR tracks

Teams may enter **up to two** Stanford XR tracks and any eligible sponsor tracks. Judges consider creativity, effort, and each track's criterion. Each Stanford XR track awards first, second, and third place; a team can win at most one Stanford XR track. The separate overall grand prize includes up to four complimentary AWE passes.

| Track | Focus | Main judging criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creative Zenith | Best of Creative XR | Artistry and craft |
| Virtuous Reality | Best of Social Good and XR | Social impact |
| AI Horizons | Best Use of AI with XR | Technical depth |
| Game Changer | Best of Gaming in XR | Fun and engagement |
| Wild West | Custom hardware or off-market parts | Technical depth, including build complexity |

**Likely team fit:** For an education or health product, target Virtuous Reality first. Consider Creative Zenith second if the experience has strong visual or interaction craft. Consider AI Horizons instead only if AI is part of the XR experience itself; using AI tools to develop the project does not by itself demonstrate a use of AI *with XR* (inference from the track description). Avoid Game Changer unless the concept is genuinely a game. Wild West is for custom or off-market hardware.

## Equipment and practical details

- **Team equipment assignment:** Snap Spectacles, reported by the team Friday evening. Confirm the exact model and developer setup at pickup. The active build should use Lens Studio.
- Loaner requests open around midday Friday after inventory check-in. Requests made before the 9:30 PM Friday match have equal priority. The equipment portal requires Google sign-in.
- Team formation forms and equipment requests are due **Friday 9:30 PM**. One equipment request per team; first matching at 9:30 PM. Matching is **not first-come first-served**; rank more equipment choices for better odds.
- The presentation lists 70+ loaner devices, including Quest 3S, Quest 2, Pico 4, Mentra Live, OpenBCI UltraCortex, XREAL Air 2 Ultra, Meta Adventurer, and Snap Spectacles.
- Bring a laptop, charger, and water bottle. Bring cables and adapters if using your own XR gear.
- Event Discord: follow #announcements; ask general questions in #event-questions. Join with the Hacker role and introduce yourself in #introduction.
- Wi-Fi: Stanford Visitor, eduroam, or Stanford for Stanford students.
- Late arrival after 10 PM Friday: email admin@stanfordxr.org as soon as possible.
