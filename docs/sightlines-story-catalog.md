Researched November 14, 2026, by eight research passes (files A–H). The full rows, with one source per fact, live in [sightlines-stories/](sightlines-stories/). This page is the index and the shortlist. The concept is in [sightlines-concept.md](sightlines-concept.md).

**Status key:**
- **V**: verified, meaning the source page was opened and states the fact.
- **S**: snippet, from a search summary only.
- **W**: Wikipedia, opened, but treated as a lead.
- **U**: unverified.

A card ships only with **V**. Distances and bearings are measured from Huang (37.42793, −122.17447). Map radius is 1.1 km, and anything farther is an edge pointer.

### Science & Engineering Quad and neighbors (file A, E)
- **Rosenkranz Quad:** renamed May 2025; four Boora-designed buildings (2008–14) that use about half the energy of conventional buildings. V
- **Huang:**
  - $30M from Jensen and Lori Huang. V
  - NVIDIA Auditorium (300 seats); Terman Library. V
  - Google, Yahoo, Cisco, Geometry Engine, Intel 4004, GeForce 256 and Knuth displays; HP workbench replica; Durand propeller; McCarty reactors. V
  - Skipped LEED and spent the fee on the green features. V
- **Y2E2:** $50M from Yang and Yamazaki (both Class of 1990); recycled gray water. V
- **Spilker:** $28M or $31M (sources conflict); GPS pioneer; underground labs; Ginzton/klystron lineage. V
- **Shriram:** $57–61M; largest of the four quad buildings, 34 labs. V. The Google-investor link is not on the Stanford page. U
- **Packard EE:** part of the $77.4M 1994 gift; Robotics Lab in the basement since 2024. V
- **Mozart I** (Snelson tensegrity) on the Packard lawn, moved 2021. V
- **Hewlett Teaching Center:** $77.4M H&P gift, $110M project, opened 1999. V (keep-out lifted)
- **Paul G. Allen:** Microsoft cofounder; his 1996 gift funded the CIS expansion by Predock; at the 1997 dedication he played guitar with D'Cuckoo. V (keep-out lifted). Gift amount S.
- **CIS origins:** Mitterrand visited in 1984; started in the McCullough basement. V
- **Gates:** $6M; Robert A.M. Stern design; united a CS department spread across eight sites; PageRank and TCP plaques; Brin's rollerblading; room 360 (S); HAI lives here. Stanley was photographed outside Gates, but put its pin at VAIL. V
- **Forsythe / Polya:** founder of CS, and CS's first home. S
- **CoDa** (opened 2025): wings shaped like 1 and 0; a stair you can decode; Fathom glass reacts to motion after dark. V. The building donor is unnamed.
- **Varian / Physics:** Bloch NMR plaque. V. Klystron in the old physics basement; Varian was the Research Park's first tenant. V
- **HEPL:** Hansen's 1947 linac ("We have accelerated electrons"). V. Gravity Probe B. W
- **McCullough:** materials science, 1965; first home of CIS. V
- **Moore Materials:** Gordon and Betty Moore, dedicated 2000. V
- **Durand:** wind-tunnel pioneer; Aero/Astro. V
- **Green Earth Sciences:** Cecil Green (TI cofounder); plaza rocks that mimic faults. W
- **Sequoia Hall:** the 1891 women's dorm became Statistics. S
- **Sapp Center:** Old Chem, one of the original buildings; closed 1989–2017. V
- **Bass Biology:** pergola modeled on gel-electrophoresis bands. V
- **Clark Center:** Foster design, round underground auditorium. V
- **Blume Center:** shake-table model from 1934 in the lobby; Blume became an engineer after the 1925 quake. V
- **Fire Truck House:** 1904; brass pole slide in 30 s; ~1,000 student firefighters; now the Women's Community Center. V
- **d.school** (Peterson, Bldg 550): $35M from Hasso Plattner; shoes-off whiteboard room. V
- **Terman Fountain/Park:** on the site of the demolished Terman Engineering Center; its beams became benches. S
- **Product Realization Lab:** descends from the 1904 student machine shops. V
