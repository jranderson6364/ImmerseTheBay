# Archive Gallery credits

Real photos for the Archive Gallery on Main Quad, Huang and Hoover (`Core/Archive.ts`). A small collage replaces the people hologram; pinching it explodes the collage into framed prints on the right, and pinching a print folds them back. Every file comes from Wikimedia Commons and is public domain or Creative Commons (CC BY / CC BY-SA). Each print carries its credit line. The collage and prints are baked by `python Retrospecs/tools/render_archive_gallery.py` from `tools/archive-photos.json`, which also regenerates `Scripts/ArchivePhotos.ts`.

Prints are adapted from the originals: very tall or wide photos are centre-cropped, levels are lifted for the additive display, and each is framed with a caption. Adapted CC BY-SA images stay under the same license.

The people holograms (`PEOPLE_HOLOGRAMS.md`) are still AI illustrations. These photos are the real thing.
